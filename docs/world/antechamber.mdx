---
title: "The Antechamber"
description: "Stone room, dormant rune-circle, sealed gate."
---

The Antechamber sits between the Sunken Choir and Bug's Hollow. A
raised stone platform dominates the center — a low altar of
cracked tiles surrounding a **dormant rune-circle** etched in
faded gold. The southern wall is a row of root-walls that won't part.

The way is sealed. The circle is the key.

## Layout

A 28-wide, 16-tall stone chamber. Mossy stone perimeter around the
central platform. Fungus pods glow in the side walls; tombstones
flank the entrance. A bloodstain marks the floor near the south
gate where someone — or something — last tried.

| Direction | Exit |
|---|---|
| **North** | Bone-gate back to the [Sunken Choir](/world/sunken-choir). |
| **South** | Fog-gate to [Bug's Hollow](/world/bugs-hollow). **Locked** until the rune-circle is lit. |

## Inhabitants

| Foe | Count |
|---|---|
| [Corrupted Grub](/enemies/corrupted-grub) | 2 |
| [Cave Spider](/enemies/cave-spider) | 2 |

The mobs flank the platform — one of each on each side.

## The switch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Walk onto the platform">
    The rune-circle is at the very center, on cracked stone. You can
    see it as a dim gold ring with a stone "eye" in the middle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step on the circle">
    Touching it lights it bright gold. The screen flashes, the
    camera shakes, and "the way south opens" floats up in
    rune-gold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk south">
    The fog-gate now reads as passable. Walking into it transitions
    to Bug's Hollow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  There is no Grace in the Antechamber. The fog-gate is one-way —
  once you walk through, you're in the boss arena until you win or
  die. Make sure you've already lit Grace #2 in the Sunken Choir
  before pressing the switch.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The switch state **persists across deaths**. Press it once, and
  the gate stays open forever — you don't have to fight the
  Antechamber mobs again to retry the boss.
</Tip>

## If the gate won't open

You haven't pressed the switch yet. Walking up to the gate without
the rune-circle lit shows the hint *"a rune-circle waits to be
lit"* in muted purple. Look for the gold platform in the middle of
the room.
