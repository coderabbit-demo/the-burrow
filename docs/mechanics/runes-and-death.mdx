---
title: "Runes & Death"
description: "What you earn, what you drop, what you lose."
---

Every kill drops **runes** — golden shards that orbit upward and add
to a counter in the top-right of the HUD. Runes are the game's
score, and they're at risk every time you die.

## Drop table

| Source | Runes |
|---|---|
| Corrupted Grub | 8 |
| Cave Spider | 14 |
| Devourer phase transition | 100 (×2 transitions) |
| Devourer kill | 600 |

A clean run from spawn to the ending tooth is around **350 + 800 = 1150
runes** if you take all the optional fights.

## Death

When your HP hits zero:

<Steps>
  <Step title='"YOU DIED"'>
    The screen fades to black with deep-red letters. Take it as a
    statement of fact, not an insult.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Runes drop where you fell">
    Every unspent rune in your counter becomes a single
    **bloodstain pickup** at the death spot. Your counter resets
    to zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respawn at last Grace">
    You wake at the Grace lantern you most recently rested at — full
    HP, full flasks, full stamina. All non-boss enemies are back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recover the bloodstain">
    Walk back to where you died. The bloodstain is a glowing
    rune-shard sprite. Touching it returns every rune you lost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Die before recovering, and the bloodstain is gone forever.** A new
  death spawns a new bloodstain at the new spot — there's only ever
  one. Greed kills runs.
</Warning>

## What survives death

These persist across deaths:

- **Bone Key** (once picked up).
- **Ember of the Hollow** (your light radius stays expanded).
- **Antechamber switch state** (if you pressed it, the gate stays open).
- **Grace progress** — your respawn point is the last Grace you lit.

## What doesn't

- Your unspent runes (recoverable once).
- Your position on the map — you go back to the last Grace.
- Boss phase progress — the Devourer fight resets to phase 1 at full HP.

<Tip>
  Light **both** Graces (Warren and Sunken Choir) before pushing the
  switch in the Antechamber. Dying in Bug's Hollow with only the
  first Grace lit means a long walk through enemies you've already
  cleared.
</Tip>
