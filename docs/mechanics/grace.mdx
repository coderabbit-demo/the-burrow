title: "Grace"
description: "The lanterns that keep you alive."
---

A **Grace** is a small candle-lit shrine on a stone base, glowing in
rune-gold. Touching one and pressing `E` makes the rabbit kneel,
and three things happen at once:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Full HP">
    Your health bar refills entirely. Every wound, every chip from a
    dodge-roll mistime — gone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3 flasks">
    Your Carrot Flasks refill back to the maximum (3). Spent ones
    return.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enemies respawn">
    Every grub and spider in the current zone is reborn. The boss is
    the exception — Bug's Hollow is one-shot, no resets.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to rest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Walk up to the lantern">
    The flame brightens slightly when you're in range. The Grace is
    surrounded by a soft golden light.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press E">
    Camera flashes gold; "GRACE LIT" floats up from the lantern. The
    rabbit kneels for about a second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stand up and continue">
    Control returns automatically. Stamina, HP, and flasks are full.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where they are

| Zone | Grace location |
|---|---|
| **Forsaken Warren** | Center of the room, on the raised mossy platform. |
| **Forgotten Alcove** | None — the alcove is a side detour, not a rest stop. |
| **Sunken Choir** | Center of the chamber, under the broken pillars. |
| **Antechamber** | None — the switch room has no Grace. |
| **Bug's Hollow** | None — you fight without one. |

<Tip>
  The Grace you most recently rested at becomes your **respawn point**.
  Lighting the second one (in Sunken Choir) means you don't lose half
  your run if you die in the boss arena.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Resting respawns enemies. If you sat down to refill flasks, you're
  about to fight everything you cleared again. Plan rest stops around
  this — usually right before a hard fight, not after one.
</Warning>
