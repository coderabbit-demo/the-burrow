The Warren is the burrow's old common chamber. Tombstones line the
edges, dead trees hold up rotted ceilings, mushrooms glow faintly
where the moisture has gotten in. Two side rooms branch off, both
held shut by tangled root-walls.

## Layout

A wide room — 32 tiles across, 17 tall — with a raised mossy stone
platform at the center. The Grace sits on the platform; the
Carrot Flask pickup sits beside it.

| Direction | Exit |
|---|---|
| **North** | Small door to the [Forgotten Alcove](/world/forgotten-alcove). |
| **South** | Root-tendril gate to the [Sunken Choir](/world/sunken-choir). Locked until you have the **Bone Key**. |

## Inhabitants

| Foe | Count | Where |
|---|---|---|
| [Corrupted Grub](/enemies/corrupted-grub) | 4 | Two near the north walls, two near the south corners |
| [Cave Spider](/enemies/cave-spider) | 2 | Patrolling the east edge and the southwest corner |

## Loot

- **Carrot Flask ×1** — on the central platform, next to the lantern.
- **Grace lantern #1** — dead center, on the platform.

## Pacing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Light Grace #1 immediately">
    Walk to the lantern, press `E`. This is your respawn point for
    everything until you light Grace #2.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull mobs one at a time">
    The Warren is open enough that you can usually engage one grub
    or one spider in isolation. Use that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Don't bother killing everything">
    Once you have the path memorized, you can run past most of the
    Warren's mobs to and from the Alcove without taking a hit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The dead trees are **solid props**. You can use them to break line
  of sight on a charging spider — useful early when your stamina
  budget is tight.
</Tip>
