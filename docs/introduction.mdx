You are the rabbit. The burrow has gone quiet in the worst way — something
has hollowed it out from below. Past the root-gates and through the bone
arch waits **The Devourer**, and only you are small enough to find it.

THE BURROW is a short, browser-based Soulslike vignette: stamina combat,
dodge-roll i-frames, lock-on, Grace lanterns as checkpoints, and a
three-phase boss at the end. One run from start to ending takes about
fifteen minutes once you know the path.
