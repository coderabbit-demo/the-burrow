---
title: "Stamina"
description: "The yellow bar is your real health."
---

Every meaningful action — attacks, dodges — costs stamina. Run out and
you can't roll, which is how most players die.

## The numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum | 100 |
| Regen rate | 60 / second |
| Regen delay after spending | 500 ms |

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Light attack (`J`) | 18 |
| Heavy attack (`K`) | 30 |
| Dodge-roll (`Shift`) | 25 |

The regen **delay** is the catch — every time you act, regen pauses for
half a second before resuming. Mash four lights in a row and you're
empty for over a second.

## Reading the bar

The yellow bar lives just under your HP bar in the top-left. Don't
look at it during a fight — look at it **between** fights. If it isn't
full when an enemy starts their windup, fall back instead of engaging.

<Tip>
  A full stamina bar buys you exactly **one** dodge **and** one heavy
  attack — your standard "panic combo". Anything less and you're
  betting on the next regen tick.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Stamina does **not** drain from movement. Sprinting away from a fight
  is always free. Use that.
</Warning>

## When it pays to wait

The Devourer's dive-slam takes ~700 ms to telegraph. That's enough time
for a full stamina top-up if you're at half. **Don't dodge early** —
stand still, watch the rear-up, then commit a single late roll. You'll
come out of the dodge with stamina to punish.
