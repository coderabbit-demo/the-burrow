Through the small door at the north of the Forsaken Warren is a stone
room about half the size of the Warren itself. The walls are webbed,
the floor stained, the air thicker. Nothing lives here. Two
spiders lurk anyway.

## Layout

A 14-wide, 9-tall stone chamber. Webs hang in the corners, a
bone pile sits at the south end, and the **Bone Key** rests on a
small altar near the north wall.

| Direction | Exit |
|---|---|
| **South** | Door back to the [Forsaken Warren](/world/forsaken-warren). |

## Inhabitants

| Foe | Count |
|---|---|
| [Cave Spider](/enemies/cave-spider) | 2 |

## Loot

- **Bone Key** — on the small platform near the north wall.

## Pacing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull one spider at a time">
    The doorway is narrow enough to bottleneck them. Back-step into
    the corridor, take one out, then advance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grab the key">
    A gold flash + "Bone Key" floats up when you collect it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk south, exit">
    No need to clear the second spider if it's still alive — sprint
    past it. The exit transition is forgiving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There's no Grace here. If you die in the alcove, you respawn at the
  Warren's lantern and the spiders are back. The alcove is a
  detour, not a waypoint.
</Note>
