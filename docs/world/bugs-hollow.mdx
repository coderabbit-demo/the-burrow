A circular pit ringed in bone. The floor is cracked stone over
something darker, and the center is stained the color of every
fight that ended here before yours. The ceiling, if there is one,
is too far up to see.

Behind you, the fog wall pulses faintly purple — the gate you came
through, no longer passable.

## Rules of the room

- **No Grace.** Dying here sends you all the way back to the
  Sunken Choir's lantern.
- **No props to break line of sight.** The arena is open. There's
  nowhere to hide; only timing matters.
- **No second chances at the Antechamber switch** — it stays
  pressed even on death, so the gate to here remains open. But
  you'll have to recover any runes you dropped on the way down.

## What waits

[The Devourer](/enemies/the-devourer). Six legs, a cracked
chitinous carapace, an eye-cluster that glows red even through
the murk. A **600 HP** boss with three phases that change how it
moves and what it can do to you.

## Reward

When the Devourer falls:

- **Tooth of the Devourer** drops at its corpse. Pick it up to
  trigger the ending card: *"The burrow is quiet again."*
- **600 runes** added to your counter (plus 200 from the two
  phase transitions).

That's the run.

<Tip>
  Save your flasks. You start with 3 and there's nothing to refill
  them with inside the Hollow. A clean phase 1 leaves you with
  full HP to absorb learning hits in phase 2.
</Tip>
