The Choir is what's left of a chamber that once mattered. Pillars
collapsed inward, bones pile near the walls, and a wet bloom of
fungus pulses in the center. The second Grace lives here, raised on
a moss-stained dais between the pillars.

## Layout

The largest of the explorable zones — 34 tiles across, 22 tall.
Four standing pillars at the corners, two ruined in the middle.
The Grace sits between them. A dried-blood corridor runs along
the bottom, leading to the Ember pickup in the southwest.

| Direction | Exit |
|---|---|
| **North** | Doorway back to the [Forsaken Warren](/world/forsaken-warren). |
| **South** | Bone-gate to the [Antechamber](/world/antechamber). Always passable — no key needed. |

## Inhabitants

| Foe | Count | Where |
|---|---|---|
| [Corrupted Grub](/enemies/corrupted-grub) | 6 | Spread across the floor, two near each pillar group |
| [Cave Spider](/enemies/cave-spider) | 4 | Two near the side walls, two ambushing in the south |

## Loot

- **Grace lantern #2** — central dais. Light it.
- **Carrot Flask ×1** — east side, near the second pillar.
- **Ember of the Hollow** — bottom-left corner, behind the
  dried-blood corridor. Permanently expands your light radius from
  150 to 210 px.

## Pacing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Beeline for Grace #2">
    The dais is the safest tile in the room. Light it before
    engaging anything else — if you die now, you respawn here, not
    in the Warren.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the south corners before the Ember">
    The Ember is in a corner. Spiders in corners are bad news.
    Clear the southwest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick up the Ember">
    Walk over it. Light radius widens immediately — you'll see more
    of the room without taking a step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="South to the Antechamber">
    Walk into the bone-gate at the bottom. No key required — the
    real lock is two zones away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The pillars block line-of-sight **and** projectiles. If the boss
  fight goes badly later and you need to stop and breathe, this is
  the room to come back to.
</Tip>
