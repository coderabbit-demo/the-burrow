---
title: "The Devourer"
description: "The boss. Three phases. 600 HP. No Grace inside."
---

A six-legged chitinous horror that has eaten the burrow from the
inside. Its carapace is dark red and cracked; an eye-cluster pulses
red at the front. It changes the way it moves at **66% HP** and
again at **33% HP** — those transitions are mandatory, hard-coded
to its health bar.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 600 |
| Speed (P1–P2) | 60 |
| Speed (P3 enraged) | 90 |
| Contact damage (P1) | 14 |
| Contact damage (P2) | ~17 |
| Contact damage (P3) | ~20 |
| Slam damage | 28 |
| Projectile damage | 18 |
| Phase 2 trigger | below 66% HP |
| Phase 3 trigger | below 33% HP |
| Runes per phase transition | 100 |
| Runes on kill | 600 |

## Phase 1 — "Skitter & Claw" (100% → 66% HP)

The Devourer chases you and throws **claw-swipes** when in melee
range, plus an occasional **dive-slam**.

### Attacks

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Claw-swipe" icon="hand">
    Telegraph: rears up briefly. Lunges 110 px in your direction
    over ~380 ms. **Counter**: dodge-roll sideways into the
    boss's flank, swing `J` twice on recovery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dive-slam" icon="bullseye">
    Telegraph: ~700 ms rear-up (longer than claw). Launches
    220 px toward you, then a shockwave deals **28 damage** in
    a 36 px radius around the impact. **Counter**: the long tell
    is your friend — wait, watch the launch direction, dodge
    perpendicular at the last moment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The dive-slam can only happen after a ~1800 ms cooldown. Once
  you've eaten one, the next ~2 seconds are slam-free — push
  damage hard there.
</Tip>

## Phase 2 — "Cracked & Calling" (66% → 33% HP)

A camera shake and red flash mark the transition. The Devourer's
carapace cracks open — the sprite changes to its enraged variant
with visible wounds. Its contact damage rises and it gains a new
attack:

<Card title="Summon Grubs" icon="users">
  Telegraph: ~700 ms rear-up identical to dive-slam, but **no**
  forward launch. Spawns **3 grubs** at random points around the
  boss (each ~30 px out). The grubs are weakened (18 HP each)
  but absolutely will tag you while you're focused on the boss.
</Card>

<Warning>
  The summon and the dive-slam share the same telegraph
  animation. The difference: the boss **doesn't move** during
  summon. If it rears up and stays put, get ready to kill grubs.
  If it rears up and lunges, dodge.
</Warning>

### Strategy

- Kill the spawned grubs immediately. Each is a 2-light-attack
  kill but their lunges interrupt your boss combo and burn
  flasks.
- The boss has a ~9 second cooldown on summon. That's enough time
  to clear adds and put real damage into the boss before the next
  call.

## Phase 3 — "Final Burst" (under 33% HP)

Another shake, another flash. The Devourer becomes **50% faster**
(speed 90, faster than your walk pace). Contact damage rises again,
and a new attack drops:

<Card title="Radial Ichor Burst" icon="explosion">
  Telegraph: ~600 ms rear-up. Fires **10 ichor projectiles** in a
  full circle from the boss's body. Each does 18 damage. Travels
  outward at 100 px/s. **Counter**: roll perpendicular to the boss
  — you only need to dodge the one or two projectiles on your
  vector, not all ten.
</Card>

<Warning>
  In phase 3 the dive-slam is, effectively, **a one-shot if you're
  full-stamina-flaskless and at low HP**. The slam is 28 damage on
  top of however many flasks you've already burned. Save at least
  one flask for the closer.
</Warning>

### Strategy

- **Lock on, strafe wide.** Most projectiles miss if you're moving
  perpendicular when the burst goes off.
- **Bait the slam, punish the recovery.** The 600 ms post-slam
  recovery is the longest opening in the fight.
- **Don't trade.** P3 contact damage is high enough that a single
  greedy hit costs you a flask.

## Reward

When the Devourer's HP hits zero, it ragdolls in place and a
**Tooth of the Devourer** drops at its corpse. Walking over the
tooth triggers the ending fade and the line:

> *The burrow is quiet again.*

You also gain **600 runes** for the kill (added to the 200 from
the two phase transitions).
