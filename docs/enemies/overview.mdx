Three enemy types in the burrow, plus the boss. Learn their tells
and they're all trivial. Misread one and you'll spend a flask.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

    Slow, fat, lunge-bites. The first thing the burrow throws at you.
    30 HP, drops 8 runes.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Cave Spider"
    icon="bug"
    href="/enemies/cave-spider"
  >
    Fast, leaps from range, bursts your stamina if it lands. 22 HP,
    drops 14 runes.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="The Devourer"
    icon="skull"

  >
    The boss. Three phases, multi-attack moveset, no respawns
    inside its arena. 540 HP.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## At a glance

| Enemy | HP | Speed | Contact dmg | Runes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corrupted Grub | 30 | 32 | 8 | 8 |
| Cave Spider | 22 | 56 | 12 | 14 |
| The Devourer (P1) | 540 | 56 | 12 | 600 + 200 |
| The Devourer (P3 enraged) | under 178 | 78 | 16 + 24 (slam) + 15 (projectile) | same |

<Note>
  "Speed" is the enemy's chase speed in the world. For reference,
  the rabbit walks at 70 and rolls at 160. You outpace grubs
  comfortably and barely outpace spiders.
</Note>
