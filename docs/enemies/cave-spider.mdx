Eight legs, dark-red carapace, a bright cluster of eyes you can see
glow even in the dim. The spider is the burrow's punisher — it
leaps from outside your sword's reach and expects you to flinch.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 22 |
| Speed | 56 |
| Contact damage | 12 |
| Aggro range | 150 px |
| Attack range | 22 px |
| Runes dropped | 14 |

## Behavior

The spider patrols its spawn point until you enter aggro range
(~9 tiles). It then scuttles toward you fast — almost matching
your walk speed — until it's about 32 px away. It then commits
to a **leap**: a 280 ms airborne dash that ignores its normal
movement rules and tracks toward where you were when it
launched.

| Phase | Tell | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Scuttle | Faster animation, eyes brighten | — |
| Leap windup | Body crouches, frame swap | ~80 ms |
| Leap travel | Airborne | 280 ms |
| Recovery | Lands, frozen briefly | ~1100 ms |

## How to kill it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The roll-in answer" icon="circle-arrow-right">
    Wait at edge of leap range. The instant the spider crouches,
    `Shift` **toward** the spider. Your i-frames overlap the
    leap; you come out behind it during its recovery. Two heavies
    finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The sidestep answer" icon="rotate">
    Lock on (`L`), strafe with `A`/`D` continuously. The leap
    tracks where you **were**, not where you'll be. Walking
    sideways at full speed makes the spider miss without using a
    dodge.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Do not block. There is no block. If the leap is going to hit, you
  must roll **before** the leap travels — once it's airborne, only
  i-frames stop it.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Spiders pull other spiders. If two are grouped, kill one
  outside the other's aggro range first — fighting both at once
  burns through stamina before either commits a leap.
</Tip>
