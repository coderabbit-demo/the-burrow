A pale-bellied carrion grub with a bright red carapace and dim
glowing eyes. Slow, predictable, and easy to clear once you've
seen the lunge once. The first creature you'll meet in the
Forsaken Warren, and the only adds the boss spawns in phase 2.

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| HP | 30 |
| Speed | 32 |
| Contact damage | 8 |
| Aggro range | 110 px |
| Attack range | 14 px |
| Runes dropped | 8 |

## Behavior

The grub idles in place until you cross its **aggro range**
(roughly 7 tiles). It then crawls toward you at a slow,
unhurried pace. Once within ~14 px it commits to a **lunge bite**:
a short hop forward with its mouth open. The bite costs you 8 HP
on contact and locks the grub in place for a beat afterward.

| Phase | Tell | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Crawl animation, no flash | — |
| Lunge | Mouth opens, body squashes back | ~220 ms |
| Recovery | Stays in bite pose, can't move | ~880 ms |

## How to kill it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The lazy answer" icon="swords">
    Walk up, swing `J` three times. Each light hit (12 dmg) chunks
    nearly half its HP. Three hits = dead. The grub's lunge
    happens once per ~1.1 s — fast attacks beat it to the punch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The clean answer" icon="circle-arrow-right">
    Bait the lunge by walking just inside aggro range, dodge-roll
    backward through the bite, then commit two heavy attacks (`K`)
    to its recovery. Dead in two hits.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Don't walk **away** from a grub when it lunges — its hop covers
  more ground than your normal walk speed. Either roll or
  out-trade. Backstepping at walk speed eats the bite.
</Warning>
