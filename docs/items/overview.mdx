Seven items, three of them mandatory for the run. Pickups float
gently and flash a gold label when collected.

## Quick reference

| Item | Effect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Carrot Flask** | Heals +60 HP. Up to 3 carried. Refilled at Grace. | Forsaken Warren, Sunken Choir |
| **Two-Heart Potion** | Heals +120 HP, capped at maximum. Carry 1; no refill at Grace. | Forsaken Warren |
| **Bone Key** | Opens the south gate of the Forsaken Warren. | Forgotten Alcove |
| **Ember of the Hollow** | Light radius widens from 150 → 210 px. Permanent. | Sunken Choir (optional) |
| **Rune Shard (bloodstain)** | Recovers all the runes you dropped on death. | Wherever you last died |
| **Tooth of the Devourer** | Triggers the ending. | Bug's Hollow, post-boss |
| **Grace Lantern** | Checkpoint. Press `E` to rest. | Forsaken Warren, Sunken Choir |

## Carrot Flask

A glowing rune-gold vial of carrot juice. Press `F` to drink — it
heals **60 HP** instantly. Each flask is one-shot.

You start with **3 flasks** and they refill the moment you rest at
any Grace. Spent flasks while clearing a zone? Walk back to the
Grace and they're whole again.

You can find one in each of the first two zones, but they're not
permanent inventory — they just refill the tank when you grab them.

<Tip>
  Drink **before** the hit, not after. The flask animation locks
  you in place for ~120 ms — try to use it when an enemy is in
  hit-stun or a boss is in recovery.
</Tip>

---

## Two-Heart Potion

A violet vial with two gold markings, found on the central platform
near the first Grace in the **[Forsaken Warren](/world/forsaken-warren)**.
Press `G` to restore **120 HP**, capped at your maximum health.

You start with none and can carry **one**. If you already have one,
the pickup stays on the ground. Drinking at full health, without a
potion, or while dead does not consume anything.

Your potion travels with you between zones. Resting preserves it
but does not refill it; death resets your count to zero. The world
pickup returns when you reload the area, like other pickups.

---

## Bone Key

A small key shaped from yellowed bone, sitting on a low altar in
the **[Forgotten Alcove](/world/forgotten-alcove)**. Walking up to
it triggers a gold flash and the label "Bone Key".

The only thing it does: lets the south gate of the
**[Forsaken Warren](/world/forsaken-warren)** part for you. After
that you'll never use it again, but it stays in your inventory
forever (and survives deaths).

---

## Ember of the Hollow

A shard of solid flame in the bottom-left of the
**[Sunken Choir](/world/sunken-choir)**, behind a dried-blood
corridor. Pick it up and your light radius **widens from 150 to
210 pixels** — you'll see roughly a third more of every room.

It's optional but recommended. The boss arena especially is much
easier to read with the ember active.

<Note>
  The ember effect is permanent. It stays expanded across all
  zones and survives death.
</Note>

---

## Rune Shard (bloodstain)

When you die with unspent runes in your counter, they coalesce
into a **single bloodstain pickup** at the spot where you fell —
a glowing rune-shard sprite that pulses gold-gold-dim.

Walk to it on your next attempt to recover **every rune you
dropped** in one pickup. Die again before reaching it and the
bloodstain is destroyed.

There's only ever **one** bloodstain at a time. A second death
moves it to the new death spot, sacrificing the previous one.

---

## Tooth of the Devourer

The boss drop. A long, jagged tooth tipped in dark red. Spawns at
the Devourer's corpse; walking over it triggers the ending
sequence:

- Camera fades to black.
- "The burrow is quiet again." rises in rune-gold.
- The run ends.

---

## Grace Lantern

A small candle-lit shrine on a stone base, glowing in rune-gold.
Not really an "item" — you don't pick it up. But touching one and
pressing `E` is the most important interaction in the game. See
**[Grace](/mechanics/grace)** for the full mechanic.

There are exactly two:
- One in the center of the Forsaken Warren.
- One in the center of the Sunken Choir.

Light both. The second is your only safety net for the boss fight.
