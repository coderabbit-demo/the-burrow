The rabbit fights with a tiny bone shortsword. Two attacks, one button each.

| Attack | Key | Damage | Knockback | Stamina | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Light** | `J` | 12 | 90 | 18 | Quick to start, quick to recover. Good for chip + dodge cycles. |
| **Heavy** | `K` | 26 | 180 | 30 | Slower, but knocks enemies back hard enough to interrupt their windups. |

Both attacks lock you in place for **240 ms**. The hitbox is a 22×22
rectangle in front of your facing — so face the enemy before you swing.

## How to read a fight

Each enemy has a clear **windup** (a frame swap or rear-up). That's your
window. Pick one:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Roll through it" icon="circle-arrow-right">
    `Shift` toward the enemy during the windup. Your i-frames carry you
    past their hit, and you come out behind them, free to swing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Out-trade with heavy" icon="hammer">
    `K` lands before most non-boss windups complete and the heavy
    knockback usually cancels their attack outright.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  You cannot cancel an attack mid-swing. If you press `J` and they
  start their windup at the same time, you're going to trade. Plan
  your openings.
</Warning>

## Hits feel real

Every successful strike does three things:

- **Camera shake** — a 60 ms tremor on light, longer on heavy and on the
  boss's slam.
- **Hit-stun tint** — the enemy flashes red for ~80 ms. They're locked
  out of attacks during this window.
- **Knockback** — pushed away in the direction your swing came from.

<Tip>
  String light attacks together to keep an enemy in hit-stun and out
  of their attack pattern. If they're flashing red, they're not biting
  you. (The spider's leap is the exception — see
  [Cave Spider](/enemies/cave-spider).)
</Tip>
