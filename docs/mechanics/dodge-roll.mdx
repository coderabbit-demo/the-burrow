---
title: "Dodge-Roll"
description: "The single most important button in the game."
---

`Shift` makes the rabbit roll. While rolling you are
**invulnerable**. Master the timing and you'll never see the
"YOU DIED" overlay.

## Timing windows

| Window | Duration |
|---|---|
| Total roll | 360 ms |
| **Invincibility (i-frames)** | **280 ms** |
| Vulnerable recovery | ~80 ms at the tail |

You're invulnerable from the **start** of the roll. The last fraction
is recovery — you're moving fast but you can be hit. Don't roll
through a sustained attack expecting to come out clean on the other
side. Roll **into** the attack so the i-frames overlap with the
strike.

## Direction

The roll uses your current movement input. If you aren't pressing
anything, it rolls in your **facing** direction.

| You're pressing | Roll goes |
|---|---|
| WASD with movement | In that direction |
| Nothing | Whichever way you're facing |

<Tip>
  In a lock-on duel, hold `A`/`D` to roll **sideways** around your
  target. Most attacks are forward cones — sidestep is safer than
  retreat.
</Tip>

## Cost

Each roll costs **25 stamina**. You can usually chain two rolls before
you're tapped out.

<Warning>
  Don't panic-roll. The 80 ms of recovery at the end of the roll is
  enough for a fast enemy to land a hit. Roll once, **stop**, then
  reassess — don't tumble across the room.
</Warning>

## When dodge-roll is the wrong tool

- Against the spider's leap if it has already left the ground — the
  spider's hitbox tracks you for too long. **Roll sideways early**, not
  late.
- When your stamina is below 25. You'll just stand there. Drink a
  flask and reset to a Grace lantern.
