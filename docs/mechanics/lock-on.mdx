---
title: "Lock-On"
description: "Glue the camera to a target and strafe."
---

Press `L` to lock onto the nearest enemy in range. A red diamond
appears above their head. Press `Tab` while locked to cycle to the
next-nearest target. Press `L` again to break the lock.

## What lock-on changes

- The lock indicator (a small red ring + chevron) is drawn on your
  target so you can keep tabs on them through the gloom.
- Your movement input becomes **strafe-relative** — `A`/`D` orbit the
  target instead of moving in absolute screen directions.
- The camera biases halfway between you and the target so you can see
  what they're about to do.

## Range

Lock-on only catches enemies within roughly **160 px** (10 tiles).
Anything further away is too dark to track anyway.

<Tip>
  Lock-on **drops automatically** when your target dies. Tap `L` again
  to grab the next-nearest enemy without skipping a beat.
</Tip>

## When to use it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Boss fight" icon="bug">
    Always. The Devourer is too fast and too big to read without the
    camera bias. Strafe with `A`/`D`, dodge through tells, hit on
    recovery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Single mob" icon="user">
    Yes, mostly. Helps with spider leaps and grub lunges where timing
    matters more than positioning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crowds" icon="users">
    Skip it. With three or more enemies, free-camera lets you respond
    to whoever's about to hit you instead of one fixed target.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Locking on **does not** auto-aim your attacks. You still need to face
  the target — the indicator helps you keep track of where "facing"
  should be.
</Note>
