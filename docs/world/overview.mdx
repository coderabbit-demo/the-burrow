The burrow is small. You can see all of it in fifteen minutes, but
you'll see it many more times than that. Zones are linked by gates and
side doors, not loaded levels — when you pass a gate the screen fades
and the next zone fades up from black.

## The path

```
                    ┌─────────────────────┐
                    │  Forgotten Alcove   │  ← Bone Key
                    └─────────┬───────────┘
                              │ (north door)
                    ┌─────────┴───────────┐
       (start) →    │  Forsaken Warren    │  Grace #1
                    └─────────┬───────────┘
                              │ (south root-gate, needs key)
                    ┌─────────┴───────────┐
                    │   Sunken Choir      │  Grace #2 + Ember
                    └─────────┬───────────┘
                              │ (south bone-gate, free)
                    ┌─────────┴───────────┐
                    │    Antechamber      │  Switch
                    └─────────┬───────────┘
                              │ (south fog-gate, needs switch)
                    ┌─────────┴───────────┐
                    │    Bug's Hollow     │  THE DEVOURER
                    └─────────────────────┘
```

## At a glance

| Zone | Enemies | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| [Forsaken Warren](/world/forsaken-warren) | 4 grubs, 2 spiders | Grace #1, Carrot Flask, side door north |
| [Forgotten Alcove](/world/forgotten-alcove) | 2 spiders | **Bone Key** |
| [Sunken Choir](/world/sunken-choir) | 6 grubs, 4 spiders | Grace #2, **Ember of the Hollow**, Carrot Flask |
| [Antechamber](/world/antechamber) | 2 grubs, 2 spiders | **Rune-circle switch** |
| [Bug's Hollow](/world/bugs-hollow) | The Devourer | Tooth of the Devourer, ending |

## Recommended run order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Light the first Grace">
    Spawn places you next to it — press `E` to register it as your
    respawn point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Detour north for the Bone Key">
    Take the door at the top of the Warren into the Alcove. Two
    spiders, one key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the south gate of the Warren">
    Walk into the root-tendril wall at the bottom of the Warren —
    with the key, it parts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Light the second Grace in Sunken Choir">
    Center of the room, between the broken pillars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: pick up the Ember">
    Bottom-left corner of the Sunken Choir, behind the dried-blood
    corridor. Widens your light radius.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cross the Antechamber and press the switch">
    Stone platform in the middle. Touching the rune-circle lights it
    gold — the southern gate becomes passable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Bug's Hollow">
    Fog-gate at the south end of the Antechamber. No going back.
  </Step>
</Steps>
