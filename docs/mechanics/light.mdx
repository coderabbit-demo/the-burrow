---
title: "Light & Vision"
description: "Why the burrow is dark, and what you can see."
---

The burrow is permanently dim. Most of the world sits behind a heavy
overlay of darkness; only soft circular halos of light reveal what's
actually around you. Reading those halos is part of the game.

## What lights what

| Source | Radius | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| The rabbit (you) | **150 px** | Always centered on the player |
| The rabbit with the Ember | **210 px** | Once you pick up the [Ember of the Hollow](/items/overview#ember-of-the-hollow) |
| Grace lantern | 60 px | At every Grace (one in the Warren, one in the Choir) |
| Fungus pod | 40 px | Sickly green pods scattered through the world |

Each halo is a smooth round fade — bright at the center, falling off
gently to the surrounding dark. Halos overlap and stack: standing on a
Grace gives you both your own halo *and* the lantern's, so the whole
local area lights up.

## What you can see outside the halos

Even outside any light source, the world isn't pitch-black — there's a
faint **ambient glow** (about 8% of full visibility) that lets distant
silhouettes still read. It's deliberately just enough to spot a
spider's red eye-cluster across a room before you walk into it, but
not enough to read the floor or hit anything.

<Tip>
  If you see two glowing red dots far ahead in the dark, that's a
  spider. Lock on (`L`) before you commit — you'll see the leap
  windup the moment it enters your halo.
</Tip>

## The Ember of the Hollow

The single most impactful pickup in the run. It permanently widens
your light radius from **150** to **210** pixels — about a third more
of every room visible at any given time, including a meaningful chunk
of the boss arena.

The Ember is **optional** — you can finish the game without it — but
it makes the boss substantially easier because you can read the
Devourer's wind-up tells from further away.

It sits in the bottom-left corner of the [Sunken Choir](/world/sunken-choir),
behind the dried-blood corridor.

<Note>
  The Ember effect is permanent. It survives death and zone
  transitions; you only need to pick it up once.
</Note>

## What about the boss arena?

[Bug's Hollow](/world/bugs-hollow) has **no Grace lantern and no
fungus pods** — just your own halo. The arena is roughly 16 tiles
across, so even with the Ember active you can't see the entire room
at once. Phase 3's radial ichor burst is harder to read because of
this — projectiles enter your halo only seconds before they hit you.

<Warning>
  Lock-on (`L`) is even more useful in the boss arena than usual —
  the camera bias slides toward the boss, effectively letting you
  "see further" in their direction at the expense of what's behind
  you.
</Warning>

## Why does it look this way?

The dark overlay is a single full-screen layer with circular holes
"erased" out of it wherever a light source exists — every halo is
a smooth, mathematically round disc. If you're seeing anything that
looks square or jagged at the edge of a light source, that's a bug,
not a feature. (We chased one such issue down already; the lighting
overlay now uses smooth bilinear sampling so the halos render
perfectly round at every zoom.)
